## Key facts (paraphrased)
- Windows 11 ships one feature update per year in H2. Enterprise, Education, IoT Enterprise and multi-session get 36 months per version; Home, Pro, Pro Edu, Pro for Workstations and SE get 24 months.
- Current end dates for Enterprise/Edu: 23H2 2026-11-10, 24H2 2027-10-12, 25H2 2028-10-10, 26H2 2029-10-09. For Pro, 25H2 ends 2027-10-12 and 26H2 ends 2028-10-10.
- 26H1 (Feb 2026) exists only for new devices. It isn't offered as an in-place update and doesn't support IoT Enterprise or hotpatch.
- Enterprise LTSC 2024 (24H2 equivalent, GA 2024-10-01) has no extended support and ends Oct 2029. IoT Enterprise LTSC 2024 has extended support to Oct 2034. LTSC is intended for special-purpose devices.
- Windows 10 support ended 14 Oct 2025, and 22H2 was its last version.
- Commercial ESU costs USD 61/device in Year One, doubling each year, for a maximum of 3 years. Years are cumulative, ESU requires 22H2, and it includes no tech support or new features.
- ESU is free for Windows 10 in W365, AVD, Azure VMs, Azure Local and similar, and Windows 10 endpoints connecting to W365 Cloud PCs are entitled for up to 3 years.
- LTSC 2024 dates differ by one day between the release-info page and the lifecycle product pages, because of time-zone notation.

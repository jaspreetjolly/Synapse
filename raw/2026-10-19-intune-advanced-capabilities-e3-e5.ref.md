# Reference — Intune advanced capabilities in M365 E3/E5 (July 2026)

Validated on the web on 2026-10-19 at Jaspreet's request. Summary in my own words, not a copy.

- **Microsoft Learn**, "Microsoft Intune advanced capabilities" (learn.microsoft.com/intune/fundamentals/advanced-capabilities): advanced capabilities (FOTA, specialty devices, Tunnel for MAM, Advanced Analytics, Remote Help, Cloud PKI, EPM, Enterprise App Management) come through Intune Plan 2, the Intune Suite, and **select Microsoft 365 bundles**.
- **Multiple secondary sources** (Cyvra, Zarioh, Paul Rigby, IT trip, HubSite 365), consistent with each other and citing Microsoft's 2026 M365 packaging and pricing FAQ and the Intune blog post "Advanced Microsoft Intune capabilities now available in Microsoft 365 E3 and E5":
  - From **1 July 2026**, rolled out tenant by tenant after 30 days' notice, complete by about **1 August 2026**.
  - **M365 E3:** Intune **Plan 2** (Tunnel for MAM, FOTA, specialty devices), **Remote Help**, **Advanced Analytics**.
  - **M365 E5:** the E3 set plus **EPM, Cloud PKI, Enterprise App Management**.
  - No action is needed to receive them. Existing Suite customers should review at renewal.

> [!question] The primary Microsoft pricing FAQ and the Intune blog were cited by these sources but not opened directly. Upgrade the evidence when one is ingested.

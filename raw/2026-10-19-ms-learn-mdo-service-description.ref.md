# Reference — Microsoft Learn: Defender for Office 365 service description

URLs, read on 2026-10-19:
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/office365/servicedescriptions/office-365-advanced-threat-protection-service-description
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/office365/servicedescriptions/microsoft-defender-for-office-365-features

Summary in my own words:
- **Plan 1:** included in Microsoft 365 Business Premium and, **effective 1 July 2026, in Office 365 E3 and Microsoft 365 E3**. E3 customers get Plan 1 capabilities only: Safe Links, Safe Attachments, anti-phishing with user and domain impersonation protection, and real-time detections.
- **Plan 2:** included in Microsoft 365 E5, A5 and G5. Adds Threat Trackers, campaign views, Threat Explorer, automated investigation and response, attack simulation training, and Defender XDR integration.
- **Secondary sources** (ITPro-Tips, Tiago S. Carvalho, Calance):
  - The rollout started on 17 June 2026.
  - Licensed users automatically get the Built-in protection preset; Standard and Strict must be enabled manually.
  - Real-time detections without Threat Explorer means the tenant is on Plan 1.

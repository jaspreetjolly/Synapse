# Summary — Defender for Office 365 Plan 1 feature map

**Source:** Jaspreet's own Markmap mind map, "Microsoft Defender for Office Plan1" ([[raw/2026-10-19-mdo-plan1-feature-mindmap.html]]). It is linked from [[summary-m365-e3-feature-map]].

## Key takeaways
1. **MDO P1 extends protection beyond email** to Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive: Safe Attachments, Safe Links and ZAP all cover Teams.
2. **P1 is protection plus basic investigation:** real-time detections and reports, without the advanced hunting or automation of a higher plan. This is my inference; the source does not list P2.
3. **Admin tooling targets a secure baseline:** Standard/Strict preset policies, Configuration Analyzer and the Tenant Allow/Block List.

## Capabilities
| Area | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| Threat protection | Safe Attachments (incl. Teams); Safe Links (incl. Teams); anti-phishing (user and domain impersonation); Safe Attachments for SharePoint, OneDrive and Teams |
| Mail hygiene | Zero-hour Auto Purge (ZAP, incl. Teams); advanced protection for internal mail |
| Threat investigation | Real-time detections; real-time reports |
| Policy and admin | Preset security policies (Standard / Strict); policy tuning; Configuration Analyzer; Tenant Allow/Block List |

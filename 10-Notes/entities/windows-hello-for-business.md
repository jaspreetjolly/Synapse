# Windows Hello for Business

Windows sign-in with a local PIN or biometrics backed by keys in TPM 2.0, instead of a password. It is the Windows building block of [[passwordless-authentication]].

- **Edition:** available on **Pro and Enterprise**. It is not an Enterprise differentiator ([[summary-windows-11-enterprise-secure-managed-cloud-ready]]).
- **Enables:** SSO to Entra ID apps, and unlocks Personal Data Encryption on [[windows-11-enterprise]].
- **Deploy via:** [[microsoft-intune]], ConfigMgr or GPO.

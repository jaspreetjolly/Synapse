# Office 365 E1 Plus

An Office 365 E1 promo bundle that adds **[[microsoft-entra-id]] P1, [[microsoft-intune]] P1 and Office 365 DLP**. It is sold only in selected emerging markets.

- **Position:** the "baseline security" tier for Copilot readiness, below [[microsoft-365-e3]] ([[copilot-security-readiness]]).
- **Productivity:** Office for the web only; 50 GB mailbox + 50 GB archive; 1 TB OneDrive.
- **Security:** EOP anti-malware and anti-spam ([[exchange-online-protection]]), DLP for email and files, manual retention labels, Cloud App Discovery via Entra ID P1.
- **AI:** Microsoft Copilot (free web chat) with [[enterprise-data-protection]]. [[microsoft-365-copilot]] is an add-on.
- **Not included (slide 7 table):** desktop Office apps, Purview P1 classification and encryption, Windows Enterprise, Defender for Endpoint P1.
- **Over plain O365 E1:** adds Intune P1 (E1 has Basic), Office 365 DLP and Entra ID P1. Storage and mailbox sizes are the same.

> [!warning] Teams inclusion conflicts between the sources. The map lists Teams; the deck says it is a separate purchase for net-new customers.

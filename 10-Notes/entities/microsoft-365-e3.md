# Microsoft 365 E3

Microsoft's enterprise bundle of productivity, identity, endpoint management and baseline security.

- **Made of:** [[office-365-e3]] + [[ems-e3]] + ME3-only extras (Windows 11 Enterprise, [[microsoft-defender-for-endpoint]] P1, Universal Print, Loop). See [[m365-licensing-stack]].
- **July 2026 uplift:** [[microsoft-defender-for-office-365]] P1 (via O365 E3), plus Intune Plan 2, Remote Help and Advanced Analytics (via EMS E3). See [[summary-m365-e3-feature-map]].
- **Detail:** [[summary-m365-e3-feature-map]]
- **Windows:** [[windows-11-enterprise]] (detail in [[summary-windows-11-enterprise-feature-map]])
- **Copilot positioning:** the "core / jumping-off point" for [[microsoft-365-copilot]] security. It sits above [[office-365-e1-plus]] (baseline) and below M365 E5. See [[copilot-security-readiness]].
- **Customer pitch example:** secure work-from-home for a government customer ([[summary-secure-wfh-government-m365-e3]]). Watch its E5-only items listed as E3 (claim-001 there).

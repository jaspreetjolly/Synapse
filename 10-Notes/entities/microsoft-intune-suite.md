# Microsoft Intune Suite

A bundle of advanced Intune capabilities on top of Intune Plan 1. **Since July 2026 (rollout complete about 1 Aug 2026), most of it is included in M365 suites:**

| Capability | M365 E3 | M365 E5 | Otherwise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intune Plan 2: **Tunnel for MAM**, FOTA, specialty devices (AR/VR, meeting rooms) | ✅ | ✅ | Plan 2 / Suite |
| Remote Help | ✅ | ✅ | Suite |
| Advanced Analytics | ✅ | ✅ | Suite |
| Endpoint Privilege Management (Windows) | ❌ | ✅ | Suite / add-on |
| Microsoft Cloud PKI | ❌ | ✅ | Suite / add-on |
| Enterprise Application Management | ❌ | ✅ | Suite / add-on |

Customers already paying for the Suite should review it at their next renewal.

> [!note] The Intune-vs-ManageEngine deck (Sept 2026) still says Tunnel and Cloud PKI are "not in E3 or E5". That is now **out of date**: Tunnel is in E3, and Cloud PKI is in E5.

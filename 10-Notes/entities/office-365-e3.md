# Office 365 E3

The productivity and baseline compliance layer of [[microsoft-365-e3]].

- **Workloads:** Office apps, Exchange Online (100 GB mailbox, 1.5 TB archive), OneDrive (5 TB), SharePoint, Teams, seeded Viva, Stream, Planner, Forms, Lists, Clipchamp, Power Platform with standard connectors.
- **Security and compliance:** DLP, anti-malware, anti-spam, eDiscovery, retention, Litigation Hold. See [[email-and-collaboration-protection-layers]].
- **Listed in the source map:** [[microsoft-defender-for-office-365]] Plan 1.

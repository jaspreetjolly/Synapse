# Office 365 E3

The productivity and baseline compliance layer of [[microsoft-365-e3]].

- **Workloads:** Office apps, Exchange Online (100 GB mailbox, 1.5 TB archive), OneDrive (1 TB default, 5 TB maximum; see [[summary-m365-e3-feature-map#claim-002]]), SharePoint, Teams, seeded Viva, Stream, Planner, Forms, Lists, Clipchamp, Power Platform with standard connectors.
- **Security and compliance:** DLP, anti-malware, anti-spam, eDiscovery, retention, Litigation Hold. See [[email-and-collaboration-protection-layers]].
- **Threat protection:** [[microsoft-defender-for-office-365]] **Plan 1**, included since **1 July 2026** ([[summary-m365-e3-feature-map#claim-005]]). Before that date, O365 E3 had only the [[exchange-online-protection]] baseline.

# Copilot+ PC

A Windows 11 PC class with a **40+ TOPS NPU**. Microsoft claims it is 5x faster than 5-year-old PCs (Cinebench 24 multi-core) and has Secured-core and **Microsoft Pluton** security by default.

- **AI features:** Recall (preview), Click to Do, Studio Effects, Live Captions (40+ languages).
- **Management:** works with existing tools. It can step up from Pro to [[windows-11-enterprise]], which unlocks advanced policies such as Recall controls.
